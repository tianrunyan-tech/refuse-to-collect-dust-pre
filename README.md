# Refuse to Collect Dust APP Pitch

An app concept that turns workout videos saved on social media into habits users actually complete.

**CN:** https://username.github.io/reponame


## User Painpoint

People save workout videos constantly and rarely do them. The act of saving delivers a sense of progress that substitutes for the behavior itself.

When a user does want to start, they hit a chain of decisions first: which platform, which folder, which video fits today, is there a better one? Most abandon the attempt and search for new content, restarting the cycle.


## Solution

Six steps, designed to remove every decision between intent and action:

1. **Import** — paste a link; title, cover, source, and platform captured automatically
2. **Auto-tag** — workout type, body area, duration generated and editable
3. **Schedule at save time** — frequency, session count, reminder in one tap
4. **Content-specific reminders** — surfaces the actual video and its length
5. **One-tap completion** — lightweight confirmation, no data entry
6. **Progress feedback** — streaks, completion rate, total time, category breakdown

## Expansion

The same pattern applies beyond fitness: recipes to cook, skills to learn, places to go. A save is an unexecuted intention. Intent-to-action generalizes across content types.
